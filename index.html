A FEW DECISIONS, EARLY

Useful by default.
Intentional by design.

  • Private by default. Listening history remains yours; shareable archives are opt-in.
  • Source-aware. Every finding links to its origin and labels what is editorial, community, or marketplace material.
  • Quality over volume. Recaps surface a few genuinely interesting trails—not a pile of search results.
